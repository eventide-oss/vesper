# Code of Conduct

Vesper adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The Covenant is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). What follows is a
summary; the full text at the link above applies.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best
  for the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory jokes.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers, using the conduct
contact listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Do not report it in a public
issue. Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, keep the
reporter's identity confidential, and may respond with a warning, a temporary
or permanent ban, or no action when none is warranted. They follow the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Covenant.
