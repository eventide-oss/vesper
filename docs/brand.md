# Brand

Vesper is the evening star, Venus at dusk: it appears, shines for a while,
and is gone before dawn. A preview does the same: it appears with a pull
request, serves it while it is reviewed, and is deleted when its time to live
ends, leaving nothing behind.

Vesper belongs to [eventide-oss](https://github.com/eventide-oss), whose logo
is a sunset with an evening star. The Vesper star is drawn on its own, with
curved branches, so that it is distinct from the straight star of the Eventide
logo, which is never cut out of it.

The name is written `vesper`, in lowercase, next to the star in the logo, in
Inter Semi Bold; and `Vesper` in sentences.

## Colors

Vesper uses the six colors of Eventide. Eventide puts Dusk, the sun, first;
Vesper puts Gold and Cream, the star, first.

| Color | Hex | Use in Vesper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | `#101026` | main background |
| Indigo | `#14142E` | surfaces and cards |
| Violet | `#4A2670` | the sky; the `Pending` phase |
| Dusk | `#F2784B` | the `Failed` phase and alerts |
| Gold | `#FFE29A` | the accent: the star, links, the `Ready` phase |
| Cream | `#FFF4D6` | text and the star |

### The colors of the phases

The same colors mean the same phase everywhere: the CLI, the site and the
docs.

| Phase | Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Pending` | Violet | the star rises |
| `Ready` | Gold | it shines |
| `Expired` | Cream at 40% | it fades |
| `Failed` | Dusk | something blocks it |

## Type

Inter for titles and text; JetBrains Mono for code, commands and labels.

## Rules

Do:

- Put the star in Cream or Gold on Night or Indigo, and in Night on a light
  background.
- Keep the curved star, distinct from the straight star of Eventide.
- Use the colors of the phases the same way everywhere.

Avoid:

- Cutting the star out of the Eventide logo to use it alone.
- Adding a moon, planets or other stars.
- A Dusk star outside of the `Failed` phase and alerts.
- Animating the star out of its cycle: it rises, shines and sets.

The source files of the identity are kept in the Figma file of eventide-oss,
by the maintainers.
